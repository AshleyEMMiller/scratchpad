The SMART Goals acronym first appeared in Management Review's 1981 issues, coined by George T. Doran. 

SMART stands for: 
- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound

It is widely used in the goal-setting and productivity world. A counter to the [[10 - Slip Box/03. Notes/SMART Goals]] system is [[10 - Slip Box/03. Notes/PACT Goals]]
